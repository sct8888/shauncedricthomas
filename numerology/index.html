Off the clock · Name numerology

What your name has been saying all along

I've been reading names for years, mostly for friends around a kitchen table. A name is the first gift anyone gives us, and I love sitting with someone and showing them the patterns hiding inside theirs.

For me, this is about faith. Numbers are one of the ways God speaks, and reading a name is really about seeing the gifts He has already placed in it.

01 / How it works

Nine numbers, one name

I use the Pythagorean system. Every letter maps to a number from 1 to 9, running through the alphabet in order and starting again after every ninth letter.

1A J S
2B K T
3C L U
4D M V
5E N W
6F O X
7G P Y
8H Q Z
9I R

Then I count how often each number appears across the full name. Every appearance is a circle of energy. The number with the most circles is the key driver, and everything flows from it. Even counts are balanced, odd counts are proactive, and any missing number is a clean slate, open to growth. When all nine are present, the name is an old soul.

Each number has its own job: 1 is the founder or leader, 2 the team, 3 money-making, 4 systems, 5 operations, 6 reflection, 7 finishing the job and faith (inviting God to play), 8 wealth and 9 giving.

A reading covers:

  • Your number grid, counted with you, letter by letter
  • Your key driver, and how it feeds every other number
  • Which numbers are balanced, which are proactive, and your clean slates
  • How it all fits together, for a person, a business or a brand

04 / Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. It's 15 minutes, there's no payment and no catch. I do it because I enjoy it.

Isn't numerology against faith?

I understand why people ask. For me it's the opposite. It's rooted in my faith and it's one of the ways I connect with God. It isn't fortune telling and there's nothing dark about it. It's a space for reflection, and it's never a substitute for professional advice.

What do I need to send?

Just the name or names you want read. For a person, the full birth name works best, plus the name you use every day if it's different. For a business, the registered name and the trading name if they differ.

How long does it take?

The reading itself is 15 minutes on a call. I'll usually reply within a few days to find a time that suits you.

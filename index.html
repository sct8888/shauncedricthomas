01 / Notes

What I'm thinking
about, lately.

Position
Updated August 2026

Over a decade in, I’ve come to think the craft is mostly about resisting the easy answer. The work I’m proudest of usually started with a number that didn’t make sense and ended with a conversation that did.

What I’m still figuring out is AI. Not the hype version — the day-to-day one. It collapses the distance between question and answer, but it also flattens judgement, taste, and the willingness to disagree with the obvious answer. The people who will do well are the ones who know what to hand to a model and what to protect from it.

And then there’s the quiet that follows the noise. Last month Anthony Fauci invoked the Fifth Amendment over a hundred times in a Senate hearing on COVID origins. Not long ago, asking questions about the vaccine, the mandates, or the origins of the virus was treated like a social swear word. Now that the official story is under oath and the answers are being refused, many of the loudest voices have gone quiet. Silence is its own kind of data.

The pattern shows up everywhere: in institutions, in marketing, in any system that prefers narrative over evidence. The work is noticing when the easy answer is doing too much of the talking.

02 / About

A few words
about me.

Bio

I'm a marketing leader based in Cape Town, working at the intersection of performance, growth, and higher education. I've helped world-class universities and institutions turn ambitious enrolment goals into measurable outcomes. UCT, MIT, Yale, LSE, Harvard, Cambridge.

At GetSmarter, a 2U brand, I'm a senior leader on the Partner Marketing team. Before that, I spent years deep inside the performance funnel: forecasting, attribution, paid media, and the unglamorous work of making numbers move. Numbers were how I learned the craft. Relationships are how I extend its reach.

Off the clock, I'm a peer-reviewed researcher, a part-time lecturer at Cape Peninsula University of Technology, and Chair of its Marketing Advisory Committee.

I'm also a long-distance runner. Two Oceans Half Marathon finisher & ultramarathon finisher. The road taught me something the office rarely does: the work that compounds is the work nobody sees.

An early look at what GPT-4 changed, what it still couldn't do, and where the technology was heading. ￼ Shaun Cedric Thomas Director, Partner Marketing at GetSmarter (a 2U brand) Recently, I've delved into the fascinating world of ChatGPT and its capabilities, and the experience has been nothing short of intriguing. With the introduction of the latest iteration, ChatGPT-4, I wanted to take this opportunity to share my insights on the remarkable advancements it brings and discuss the potential benefits it could offer to you. GPT-4 boasts several key advancements over its predecessor. Language understanding has stepped up In the realm of AI language understanding, GPT-4 has surpassed its predecessor, GPT-3.5, by demonstrating a remarkable ability to comprehend and generate text in various dialects and respond empathetically to emotions in the text. By recognising and reacting sensitively to emotions such as sadness or frustration, GPT-4 creates a more authentic and personal interaction. This advanced model is exceptionally versatile in understanding regional or cultural variations in language due to its ability to tackle dialects with unique vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation. It can answer complex questions properly GPT-4 excels at answering complex questions by integrating information from multiple sources, an area where GPT-3.5 often falls short. For example, GPT-4 can provide detailed and nuanced responses regarding the connection between declining bee populations and global agriculture by referencing a variety of studies and sources. Additionally, GPT-4 can cite its sources accurately, allowing readers to easily verify the information presented. Creative output is more coherent GPT-4 outshines GPT-3.5 in producing creative content, such as stories, poems, or essays, while maintaining coherence and creativity throughout. Unlike GPT-3.5, which may falter in maintaining narrative consistency, GPT-4 can create short stories with well-developed plots and character development. It handles maths and science GPT-4 showcases a superior capacity to solve complex mathematical and scientific problems compared to GPT-3.5. It can tackle advanced calculus problems and simulate chemical reactions, among other tasks. GPT-4's deep understanding of complex mathematical and scientific concepts spans subjects like physics, chemistry, biology, and astronomy. With its advanced processing power and language modeling capabilities, GPT-4 can analyse intricate scientific texts and provide clear insights and explanations. Code generation is now genuinely useful GPT-4 has gained attention on social media for its capacity to generate code snippets or debug existing code more efficiently than GPT-3.5, making it an invaluable asset for software developers. GPT-4's ability to condense weeks of work into mere hours leads to exceptional results at an unprecedented speed. It can see Unlike GPT-3.5, which focuses primarily on text, GPT-4 can analyse and comment on images and graphics. It can describe the content of a photo, identify trends in a graph, or even generate captions for images, positioning itself as a vital tool for education and content creation. And it's safer GPT-4 implements mechanisms to minimise undesirable outcomes, thereby increasing its reliability and ethical responsibility. As a result, it is less likely to generate politically biased, offensive, or harmful content, making it a more reliable AI companion compared to GPT-3.5. Where ChatGPT still has room to grow While ChatGPT's advancements are impressive, there is still room for improvement in several areas: Contextual neutrality. Enhancing its ability to discern context and respond accordingly. User understanding. Developing the capacity to understand the communicator's identity (who, where, and how). External integrations. Expanding its reach through web, API, and robotic integrations. Long-term memory. Improving its ability to recall past interactions and apply that knowledge to future conversations. Reducing hallucination. Minimising instances where the AI is convinced of false information. As ChatGPT continues to evolve, it is poised to revolutionise marketing and AI-driven communications. Its potential applications in content creation, education, customer service, and more make it an essential tool for businesses and individuals navigating the digital age.

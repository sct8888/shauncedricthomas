Tarot cards: discovering the surprising link between tarot and digital marketing.

An exploration of the unexpected parallels between tarot symbolism and the modern, data-driven world of digital marketing.

Shaun Cedric Thomas
Director, Partner Marketing at GetSmarter (a 2U brand)

In today's fast-paced world, being open-minded is a necessity rather than an exception. This article is for those who are open to exploring unconventional connections and ideas that might enhance their digital marketing strategies. As someone who started my personal journey with tarot just over six years ago, I can attest to the transformative power of these mystical cards (those who know me well can vouch for my journey with them). So, I began to wonder: is there a link between tarot cards and digital marketing?

Tarot cards, an ancient tool often associated with divination and fortune-telling, might seem like an unlikely source of inspiration for digital marketing strategies. However, with creativity and an open mind, there are fascinating parallels that can be drawn between the mystical realm of tarot cards and the modern, data-driven world of digital marketing. In this article, I will explore the unique connection between these two seemingly unrelated fields and consider whether a study on this topic might reveal hidden secrets for marketing success.

The Journey of the Fool: customer journey mapping

In the tarot deck, the Fool card represents the beginning of a journey, filled with potential, curiosity, and unexpected encounters. This card's symbolism can be likened to the customer journey in digital marketing. The process through which consumers discover, evaluate, and ultimately decide to purchase a product or service.

Just as the Fool embarks on a voyage of self-discovery, digital marketers must map out their customer's journey, understanding their motivations, desires, and pain points. By incorporating the tarot's concept of the Fool's journey, marketers can create a more comprehensive and empathetic customer journey map that helps them anticipate and address customer needs.

Tarot archetypes: creating compelling brand personas

Tarot cards feature a variety of archetypes, such as the Magician, the High Priestess, and the Emperor, each representing different aspects of human nature and experience. These archetypes can serve as inspiration for creating brand personas in digital marketing.

By aligning a brand with a specific tarot archetype, marketers can craft a distinctive brand voice and messaging that resonate with their target audience. For example, a brand that embodies the characteristics of the Magician (creativity, innovation, and transformation) can appeal to consumers seeking cutting-edge solutions or unique experiences.

The Wheel of Fortune: embracing change and adaptability

The Wheel of Fortune card in tarot symbolises the cyclical nature of life, constant change, and the importance of adapting to new circumstances. Similarly, the digital marketing landscape is constantly evolving due to technological advancements, emerging trends, and shifting consumer behaviour.

Embracing the lessons of the Wheel of Fortune, marketers must remain agile and open to change, adapting their strategies to capitalise on new opportunities and maintain a competitive edge.

A study worth pursuing?

While the connection between tarot cards and digital marketing may seem unconventional, there are valuable insights and lessons to be gained from this mystical art form. A study exploring the application of tarot principles to digital marketing could uncover novel approaches, encouraging marketers to think outside the box and unlock new levels of creativity and success.

By considering the symbolism and wisdom of tarot cards, digital marketers can gain a fresh perspective on their strategies, enhance their understanding of customer behaviour, and embrace the ever-changing digital landscape with curiosity and adaptability.
